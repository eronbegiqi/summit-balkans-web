## What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device when you visit our website. They help us provide a better experience by remembering your preferences and analyzing how you use our site.

## Types of Cookies We Use

### Essential Cookies (always active)

Required for the website to function properly. These cannot be disabled.
- Session cookies (login state, booking flow)
- Security cookies (CSRF protection)
- Cookie consent preferences

### Analytics Cookies (optional)

Help us understand how visitors use our website.
- Google Analytics 4 — page views, traffic sources, user behavior
- Hotjar — heatmaps and session recordings (anonymized)

### Marketing Cookies (optional)

Help us deliver relevant content and measure campaign effectiveness.
- Meta Pixel — Facebook/Instagram ad tracking
- Google Ads — conversion tracking

## How to Manage Cookies

You can manage your cookie preferences at any time:
- Click the "Cookie Settings" link in the footer
- Adjust browser settings to block cookies
- Use the cookie banner displayed on first visit

Disabling some cookies may affect website functionality.

## Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are set by third-party services we use:
- Google (Analytics, Ads)
- Meta (Facebook Pixel)
- Stripe (payment processing)
- Hotjar (analytics)

Each has its own privacy policy. Refer to their websites for details.
