## Introduction

By making a booking with Summit Balkans, you enter into a legally binding agreement and acknowledge that you have read, understood, and accepted these conditions in full. A contract between the Traveler and Summit Balkans is established once a booking request has been accepted in writing (via email or official confirmation) and the required deposit or full payment has been received.

From that moment, these Booking Conditions apply to all services and all participants included in the booking. The contract is considered binding regardless of whether the Traveler has physically signed a document.

The person making the booking confirms that they are authorized to accept these conditions on behalf of all members of their group. It is their responsibility to ensure that all participants are fully informed of these terms, including any risks, obligations, and participation requirements associated with the tour.

To initiate a booking, Travelers must submit a formal request through official communication channels such as the Company's website, email, or authorized representatives.

Travelers are required to provide accurate and complete personal information, which may include:

- Full name (as per passport)
- Date of birth and nationality
- Passport details (where required for permits or border crossings)

- Relevant medical conditions or dietary requirements
- Any other relevant information required for the booking by Summit Balkans

All bookings are subject to availability and operational feasibility and are not guaranteed until confirmed in writing by Summit Balkans.

The Company reserves the right to decline a booking at its discretion, particularly where safety, logistical, or operational concerns arise, or where incomplete or inaccurate information has been provided.

## 3. Prices

All prices are quoted in Euro (EUR) and are based on the itinerary, services, group size, and logistical arrangements specified at the time of booking.

Prices include only those services explicitly listed in the confirmed itinerary. Any services not clearly stated—such as personal expenses, beverages, optional activities, or additional services—are excluded unless otherwise agreed in writing.

Summit Balkans reserves the right to adjust prices prior to final payment in the event of significant changes in external costs, including but not limited to:

- Fuel or transportation costs
- Exchange rate fluctuations
- Government-imposed taxes or fees
- Changes in supplier pricing

Any such adjustments will be communicated transparently and without delay. Once the final balance has been paid, no further surcharges will be applied.

## 4. Deposit & Payment

A non-refundable deposit of 20% of the total tour price is required to confirm a booking. This deposit secures the Traveler's place and enables the Company to proceed with reservations and operational planning.

The remaining balance must be paid no later than 30 days prior to the departure date. For bookings made within 30 days of departure, full payment is required within 48 hours of confirmation.

Payments must be made using the methods specified by the Company. Any bank charges or transfer fees are the responsibility of the Traveler.

Failure to meet payment deadlines may result in cancellation of the booking, with applicable cancellation fees applied as outlined below.

## 5. Cancellation by Traveler

All cancellations must be submitted in writing and will take effect on the date they are received by Summit Balkans.

Cancellation fees are applied to compensate for costs already incurred and commitments made with accommodation providers, transport services, and local partners:

- **More than 45 days before departure:** loss of deposit
- **44 to 30 days before departure:** 30% of total price
- **29 to 15 days before departure:** 60% of total price
- **14 days or less before departure (including no-show):** 100% of total price

No refunds will be issued for unused services once the trip has commenced. This includes missed accommodations, transfers, activities, or early departure from the tour.

Travelers are strongly encouraged to obtain travel insurance to cover cancellation costs.

## 6. Changes by Traveler

Travelers may request changes to their booking, such as travel dates, participant names, or selected services. All such requests must be submitted in writing.

Summit Balkans will make reasonable efforts to accommodate changes, subject to availability and operational constraints.

Minor changes may be permitted without additional charge if requested sufficiently in advance. Subsequent changes, or changes involving third-party services (such as accommodation or transport), may incur administrative fees and additional supplier costs.

Changes requested close to the departure date may not be feasible and may be treated as a cancellation and rebooking under the applicable cancellation terms.
